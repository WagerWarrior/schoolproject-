STUDENT DROPOUT PROJECT — DECISION LOG
Updated November 21, 2026

PURPOSE
Test whether first-semester results improve classification of recorded dropout outcomes compared with enrollment information alone. Missing dropout dates mean this is not validated forecasting of future dropout.

STARTING COLUMN PLAN
Model A: 11 enrollment fields, listed below.
- Course
- Age at enrollment
- Application mode
- Application order
- Daytime/evening attendance
- Previous qualification
- Mother's qualification
- Father's qualification
- Mother's occupation
- Father's occupation
- Displaced

Model B: all Model A fields plus six first-semester fields:
- Curricular units 1st sem (credited)
- Curricular units 1st sem (enrolled)
- Curricular units 1st sem (evaluations)
- Curricular units 1st sem (approved)
- Curricular units 1st sem (grade)
- Curricular units 1st sem (without evaluations)

These are 11 and 17 source columns before category encoding. Use the same students, splits and preprocessing policy for both versions. The feature list is a starting plan, not a claim that each field will prove useful. Parents' education and occupations can act as socioeconomic proxies; review subgroup errors and interpretation.

TARGET
Target is the answer, never an input: Dropout = 1, Enrolled or Graduate = 0. Enrolled does not mean guaranteed graduation. Keep the original outcome column and derive a separate binary label.

EXCLUSIONS FROM PRIMARY MODELS
- All six second-semester fields: later information outside our chosen input window.
- Marital status, nationality, special needs, gender and international status: retain for aggregate fairness review; exclude from primary inputs pending instructor review.
- Debtor, tuition payment status and scholarship status: exclude until prediction-time availability is verified. Any entry-only model input must be available at enrollment.
- Unemployment, inflation and GDP: exclude until reference period and availability are documented; GDP also needs unit clarification.
Keep all fields in the original file. Excluding a model input does not mean deleting it from the source data.
